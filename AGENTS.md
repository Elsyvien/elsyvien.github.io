## Design Context

### Users
Technical peers, recruiters, collaborators, and people interested in Max's software and photography. The site should make his work easy to understand without reading like a product pitch.

### Brand Personality
Precise, understated, and personal. Confident through specificity and craft, not bold claims or inflated language.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial and minimal, with strong typography, generous space, a cool blue accent, and both light and dark themes. Preserve the original photography gallery as the main expressive interaction: a directly draggable track with strong image parallax. Avoid orange accents, generic portfolio slogans, decorative tech clichés, and marketing-heavy copy.

### Design Principles
1. Prefer factual, direct headings and short copy.
2. Use blue consistently as the sole interface accent.
3. Keep the gallery tactile and distinctive; match the original drag physics and parallax depth.
4. Mention concrete experience where it adds context, without repeating it across the page.
5. Preserve accessibility, responsive images, reduced-motion support, and clear keyboard interaction.
